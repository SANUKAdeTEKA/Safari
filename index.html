Wilpattu Safari Adventures

Wilpattu National Park is Sri Lanka's largest and most pristine wildlife sanctuary, famous for its leopard population and unique "villus" (natural lakes). Our expert guides will take you on unforgettable safari experiences through this remarkable ecosystem.

Morning Safari

Depart at dawn when wildlife is most active. Spot leopards, elephants, and more with our expert trackers.

Full Day Expedition

Deep exploration of Wilpattu's remote areas with picnic lunch at a scenic villu (natural lake).

Night Safari

Exclusive after-hours experience to spot nocturnal species rarely seen during daylight.

Wilpattu's Wildlife

Wilpattu National Park is home to an incredible diversity of flora and fauna. Spot these magnificent creatures during your stay with us.

Sri Lankan Leopard

Wilpattu has one of the highest leopard densities in Sri Lanka, with frequent sightings near our cabanas.

Asian Elephant

Herds of elephants are commonly seen bathing in the park's many villus (natural lakes).

Sloth Bear

This rare and elusive species can sometimes be spotted foraging in the early mornings.

Birdlife

Over 200 species including the Sri Lanka junglefowl, painted stork, and crested serpent eagle.
